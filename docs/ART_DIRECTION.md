# Art direction — Visual Quality Pipeline (V2)

## The visual contract

Project Atlas targets a **readable, original, late-1990s pre-rendered isometric adventure aesthetic**, not a literal copy of Little Big Adventure. The reference describes craft and readability, **not licensed content**. Do not extract or trace the game's sprites, textures, room layouts, characters, models, palette or animations.

### Golden sample: Field Telemetry Receiver

The first golden sample is an original portable embedded/RF field receiver: a compact die-cast case, offset antenna, recessed signal display, mechanical knobs, protective guards, connector array and carrying grip. Its shape intentionally differs from any named LBA prop.

The object must read immediately as *electronic field equipment* when displayed without a caption.

| Feature | Design rule | Verification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary silhouette | Rectangular cast body, offset mast/antenna, grip, non-symmetric side controls | Distinct silhouettes NE/NW/SW/SE at 192–256px |
| Volume and bevels | Rounded/beveled body edges visibly catch the key light; no razor-flat cuboid edges | Edge highlight survives reduction |
| Front hierarchy | Strongly recessed deep-navy screen with teal waveform and three physical pushbuttons | Display and controls separable on 192px frame |
| Materials | Satin marine enamel, dark elastomer, brushed anodized alloy, warm copper fittings, emissive teal display | Different roughness/metalness and luminance per region |
| Small details | Knurled knob, real screws, recessed ports, ventilation slots and labels | Only physically resolvable details survive; avoid pixel noise |
| Palette | Original designed range: navy #253f51, teal #6bd7c7, copper #dc8953, warm bone #d3c4a5, shadow #102631 | Coherent across camera directions; not extracted from LBA |
| Lighting | Large warm key upper-left/front, cool low-energy fill and restrained rim; neutral world | Highlights stay directional and read as volume |
| Framing | Stable shared orthographic scale and world pivot; no crop per direction | Compare common-origin centers between frames |
| Background | RGBA transparent final sprites; background/ground shadow rendered separately when used | No baked opaque backdrop or dark alpha halo |
| Asset ownership | All geometry, shaders and decals generated by our own Blender fixture scripts or owner-produced files | Rights evidence required before any public approval |

### Numeric targets (initial, not aesthetic substitutes)

- Gold-sample Blender source: 768 × 768 px as a production candidate; 512px remains a diagnostic control.
- Final output comparisons: **192, 224, 256px**; **truecolor, 96, 160, 256 colors**.
- Target vertical occupancy: ~0.68–0.90 of frame *with consistent camera origin*; avoid clipping tall antenna.
- Shared camera direction azimuth and elevation for all assets within one scene; no variable lighting between rotation views.
- Preserve the renderer's fractional alpha and premultiply colors during resampling to avoid dark fringes.
- No quality claim solely from color-count, PSNR, or edge metrics. A named human review of four views at in-game scale is mandatory.

## Evaluation order

1. Inspect four original Blender frames at **native size and at 192px**; identify screen, buttons, mast and grip before seeing text.
2. Compare matte / metallic / rubber / emissive appearance; beware of pure black crushing screen details.
3. Compare alternative color conversions **against a truecolor baseline from the same rendered geometry**.
4. View the same sprites on 3 backdrops (dark, medium and light); verify alpha and stable position.
5. Record preference and actual reasons with screenshot evidence. Only then freeze presets.

## Explicit scope boundary

V1 diagnostics and V2 art direction are documentation/evaluation gates. V3 modifies the original model source and V4 its rendering. V5 exports a reversible **comparison matrix**, not production-approved assets and not a deployment to GitHub Pages. V6–V8 (UI comparisons, scene integration, preset approval) require a separate go.

Engineering fixture renders remain marked **PENDING artistic review**, even after successful CI.
