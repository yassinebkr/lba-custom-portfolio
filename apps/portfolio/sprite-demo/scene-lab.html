TRUE ORIGINAL ASSETS

A real 3D room, not painted Canvas blocks

The floor, two walls, instrument shelf, workbench, controls, and loose workshop equipment are independently modeled and lit in Blender. The radio itself uses the exact V5 256 px/Lanczos atlas, composited between the background and the desk-lip render.

LIMITATIONS

A visual QA environment

Placement movement is intentionally limited to the tabletop. The radio's shadow is an approximation, not yet a physically rendered cast-shadow pass. This is a test for V7; it is not a finished playable room, navigation system or V8 production preset.
